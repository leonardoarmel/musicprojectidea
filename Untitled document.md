Goal: Find patterns in popular music

Steps:  
1\. Download several songs  
2\. Have Claude write a tool to analyse them  
3\. Look at the trends and see if there is a correlation between the song’s genre and the motifs  
4\. Write it up

Background: Many genres of songs have overarching motifs (such as the four chords in rock music). Idea of this project is to look for these motifs in a variety of genres and potentially identify what makes them work.
